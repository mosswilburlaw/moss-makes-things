Effective May 9, 2026 · Last updated May 9, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Moss Makes Things ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you interact with our content on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and other platforms, or use any application we publish that integrates with those platforms via their public APIs.

1. Information We Collect

Through platform APIs (e.g. TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram Graph API, YouTube Data API), we may receive: account identifiers, public profile data, content we publish on your behalf when you grant authorization, and analytics data the platform exposes to authorized apps.

We do not collect government identifiers, payment information, biometric data, precise location, or contents of private messages.

2. How We Use Information

To publish content you have authorized us to publish, to read public engagement metrics on that content, and to maintain technical logs for debugging and platform-policy compliance.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We share data only with the originating platform (per its API terms) and with infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the service.

4. Data Retention

API tokens are retained only as long as needed to fulfill authorized publishing tasks. You may revoke our access at any time through the originating platform's app-permissions settings, which immediately invalidates our tokens.

5. Children

Our service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will reflect the latest version.
